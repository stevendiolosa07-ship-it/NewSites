Text Messaging Terms

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This page explains the text messages sent by NewSites.com ("we," "our," or "us") and how to start, stop, or get help with them.

Text us

You can start a conversation by texting (229) 592-4933. By texting that number you agree to receive text message replies from NewSites.com about your request, on the terms below. You can also call the same number.

What messages you will receive

We send text messages only to people who have texted or called us, or who have asked us to contact them. Messages include:

  • Replies to your questions, sent by our AI messaging assistant and our team
  • Follow-ups about the request you made, such as setup, scheduling, or booking details
  • A text-back when we miss your call

We do not send promotional or marketing text messages from this program. Businesses that use NewSites.com may also send messages from their own numbers to people who have contacted them. Those messages are covered by the same opt-out rules.

Consent

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of buying any product or service. You consent by texting us first or by giving your mobile number on a form that tells you we will text you.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies with your conversation. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opt out, opt back in, and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation message and then no more.
  • Reply START to opt back in after you have opted out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email stevendiolosa07@gmail.com.

Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We cannot guarantee delivery.

Your information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the providers that help us deliver messages, such as our messaging carrier.
