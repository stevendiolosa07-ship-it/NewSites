Enterprise Custom AI

We don't replace your tech stack. We supercharge it.

Plug customized, autonomous AI directly into your existing CRMs, relational databases, and corporate pipelines — erasing up to 90% of operational overhead. One architecture fee, then a system that keeps compounding.

01Dynamic Cost-Arbitrage Engine

Our system acts like a smart traffic controller for your AI tasks. It routes simple, everyday jobs to lightning-fast, cheap models and only wakes up the heavy, expensive models for complex analysis. This slashes your monthly AI bills by up to 70% while keeping system speeds blazing fast.

02Executive Guardrail Gateways

Safety first. Before the AI fires off a mass email campaign, alerts thousands of leads, or schedules a payment, the system builds the draft entirely but pauses for your 1-click 'Executive Approval.' You maintain absolute control over everything the system does.

03Interactive Operational Skill Tree

A live visual dashboard that shows you exactly what your AI system knows how to do right now, what it is currently learning, and what advanced skills will unlock next as your business scales month-over-month.

04Autonomous Lead Scoring

The AI immediately reads every incoming lead's profile, intent, and background data, sorting the hot buyers from the window shoppers so your sales team only calls people who are ready to close.

05CRM Data Mapping

No more messy data or manual entries. The system automatically reads, organizes, and updates your entire customer database across all your apps in real-time, keeping your pipeline perfectly clean.

06Multi-Agent Collaboration

Think of this as an army of specialized digital employees. A lead-capture AI talks seamlessly to a scheduling AI, which passes info to a data-enrichment AI—all working together behind the scenes to close deals.

07Legacy API Hooking

We don't force you to change how you work. Our system seamlessly hooks into your old, existing tools and legacy software, breathing new life into your current tech stack without a costly 'rip-and-replace.'

08Instant Auto-Nurture

The second a customer reaches out—day or night—the system instantly responds with hyper-personalized answers, keeping your prospects warm and moving them down the sales funnel on autopilot.

09Real-Time Pipeline Enrichment

As soon as a new contact drops into your pipeline, the AI scrapes public records, corporate data, and social metrics to instantly give your team a full dossier on who the buyer is before you even speak to them.

10Document Intake & Extraction

Erase manual data entry. Upload complex contracts, invoices, tax forms, or spreadsheets, and the system instantly pulls out the exact figures, key terms, and summaries you need in seconds.

11Custom Systems & Integrations

Your business is unique. This is our blank-canvas node where we build bespoke, highly intricate tools engineered entirely around your company's proprietary operational workflows.

12Finance AI Engine

Your books get a tireless analyst on staff. The system reads live revenue and expense data to forecast cash flow weeks ahead, recognizes revenue automatically as it's earned, and keeps the ledger reconciled in real time — so you see a cash crunch coming before it hits, not after.

13Project Management AI BETA

Every project gets a running scoreboard. The system tracks true profitability per project as costs and hours roll in, automates routine task handoffs, and flags when a team member is overloaded — catching a project that's bleeding margin while there's still time to fix it.

14Custom Reporting & Forecasting

Skip the spreadsheet marathon. Build any custom report straight from live data in minutes, hand execs a KPI dashboard that updates itself, and run automated scenario models before you commit budget — no analyst required to pull the numbers first.

15Automated Asset & Revenue Engine

Fixed assets stop falling through the cracks. The system auto-tracks every asset from purchase through depreciation and keeps compliance reporting audit-ready year-round, so year-end doesn't turn into a scramble to reconstruct twelve months of paperwork.

Questions, answered

How fast can my business be up and running?+
Most setups go live within 24 hours. We configure your AI calling, messaging, and CRM around how you already work, so there's no long onboarding to sit through.
Do I have to let the AI run on its own?+
No. You choose the level of automation. The AI can handle calls, texts, and follow-ups fully on autopilot, or queue everything for your approval first — it's your call, and you can change it anytime.
Will it work with the tools I already use?+
Yes. Leads, calls, and messages flow into one place and sync with your calendar and existing workflow, so you're not juggling separate apps or spreadsheets.
What does it cost?+
There are three plans — Starter, Growth, and Empire — sized to where your business is today. Tell us where you'd like to start below and we'll recommend the right fit.
Is my data secure?+
Your conversations and customer data are handled over secure connections and stay tied to your account. We don't sell your data or share it with third parties.
