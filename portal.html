        Auto-AION

        The master switch. When it is off, your bots stop acting on their own in every section below. Nothing is deleted, and each section keeps its own setting for when you turn it back on.

        Where autonomous AI is allowed

        Turn off any section to take over that work yourself. Changes apply to new activity right away.

        Opt-out requests (STOP) and HELP replies are always honored, even when Auto-AI is off. Messages already scheduled before you turn a section off are skipped, not sent late.

        AI Help Desk

        Ask how your leads, call and text logs, and bot settings work. The assistant knows this portal and your NewSites features. It can explain and troubleshoot, but it can't change your account.

        AI answers can be wrong. For billing, legal or account changes, email stevendiolosa07@gmail.com. Help desk conversations may be saved to improve support.

        Privacy Policy

        Last updated October 19, 2026. This is the version for portal users. The full public policy is at privacy.html.

        1. Who this covers

        NewSites.com ("we") provides AI-powered call answering, texting, lead management and social posting for businesses. "You" means the business that holds this portal account. "Your contacts" means the people who call, text or submit their details to your business.

        2. Data we handle

        • Account data: your name, business details, email, phone number and portal access token.
        • Lead data: your contacts' names, phone numbers, emails, messages, lead scores and pipeline status.
        • Call data: call logs, recordings where enabled, and transcripts.
        • SMS/MMS data: message content, phone numbers, delivery status, and records of opt-in and opt-out.
        • Connected accounts: access tokens for social platforms and other tools you link.
        • Usage data: log data, IP address and browser type.

        3. Text messaging (SMS/MMS)

        We send text messages only to people who contacted your business or agreed to be texted. Every contact can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help, and we honor opt-outs automatically. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties except the providers that deliver messages, such as our carrier. See the Text Messaging page.

        4. AI interactions

        Our bots draft replies, summaries and posts using AI. To do this, the content of calls, texts and forms may be sent to AI model providers for processing. AI output can be wrong or incomplete, so review the settings and content you rely on. The Auto-AI controls in this Settings area let you decide where bots may act without your approval. Questions typed into the AI Help Desk are processed the same way and may be saved to improve support.

        5. Third-party services and integrations

        We share data only as needed to run the service, with providers such as our phone and SMS carrier (Twilio), AI model providers, email and payment providers, and any CRM, calendar or social platform (for example Meta, Google, X) you choose to connect. Those services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

        6. Your responsibilities

        You are responsible for having the consent the law requires before your contacts are texted or called, for honoring opt-outs, for telling contacts when they are speaking with an AI assistant where the law requires it, and for call-recording notices that apply where you operate.

        7. Retention, security and your rights

        We keep data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No system is completely secure. You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or your contacts' data, by emailing us.

        8. Contact

        NewSites.com, stevendiolosa07@gmail.com. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
